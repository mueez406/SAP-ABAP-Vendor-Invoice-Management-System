PAID INVOICES ALV
=================

Purpose
-------

The Paid Invoices report displays invoices whose current status
is PAID.

Data Source
-----------

Table: YMINVOICE

Selection Condition
-------------------

The program selects invoices where:

STATUS = 'PAID'


ALV Display
-----------

The report uses:

REUSE_ALV_GRID_DISPLAY

The ALV displays invoice information from the YMINVOICE table,
including the invoice details and payment information.


Report Title
------------

PAID INVOICE


Business Logic
--------------

When the complete invoice amount has been paid:

Balance = 0

The program sets:

STATUS = 'PAID'

The invoice is then displayed in the Paid Invoices report.


Purpose for End Users
---------------------

This report allows users to:

1. View invoices that have been completely paid.
2. Check the invoice amount.
3. Check the total paid amount.
4. Check the remaining balance.
5. Verify the payment status.
