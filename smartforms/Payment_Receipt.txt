------------------------------------------------------------

Integration:

The Smart Form is connected to the RECEIPT section/button
of the main ABAP report YMINVOICE_M.

The user enters an Invoice ID and selects the PRINT button.

The program retrieves the invoice details from YMINVOICE
and the related vendor details from YMVENDOR.

The Smart Form used by the project is:

ZYM_PAYMENT_RECEIPT
